Here is my restaurant plan on one page: what I need, what it costs, and how many guests I need to break even. All figures are my own estimates, in rupees.

The idea

A small 30-seat kitchen in Betalbatim, South Goa: momos, thukpa, wok dishes and a few Korean dishes. Food only, no bar. I found no other Korean place within 6 km of the spot, and the momo stalls nearby sell 7 pieces for about ₹100, so I will compete on quality, not on price.

What I need (one time, ₹ lakh)

  • Rent deposit (3 months)1.20
  • Interiors and furniture3.75
  • Kitchen equipment2.20
  • Licences and signboard0.40
  • Billing app, menus, launch0.60
  • Plates, cutlery, first stock1.00
  • Set-up total9.15 lakh
  • My backup cash (see below)3.35 lakh

Total I need: ₹12.5 lakh

A 30-seat room costs more to fit out than a 24-seat one: more furniture and plates.

My backup

₹3.35 lakh kept aside, about 3 months of fixed costs. June to August is slow in Goa, and I expect only a small loss then, about ₹7,000 a month. If only half the guests I plan for turn up, I would still end the year slightly ahead (about ₹0.7 lakh), though my worst months would lose around ₹59,000 each. The backup can cover that.

What it costs every month

  • Rent40,000
  • 3 staff: chef, helper, server50,000
  • Gas, power, water, internet13,000
  • Repairs, licences, marketing8,000
  • Fixed total1,11,000

On top of that, ingredients are about 30% of each sale (35% in the rainy months) and card fees about 2%. So out of every ₹100 sold, about ₹68 is left to pay the monthly costs.

Break-even

I need about ₹1.63 lakh of sales a month, which is about 10 guests a day at ₹550 each, only a third of the 30 seats. In the rainy months it is about 11 guests (₹1.76 lakh). Below that I lose money, above it I earn.

Payback

  • If all goes to planabout 10 months
  • My planning caseabout 15 months

Time until the whole ₹12.5 lakh comes back as profit: ₹14.7 lakh a year if all goes to plan, ₹9.85 lakh a year in my planning case. Average months, before my own pay and tax; the real date depends on the season I open in.
