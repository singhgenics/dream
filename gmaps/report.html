The short version

A real gap, a crowded floor, one strong rival

Nobody near the pin combines Chinese, Tibetan and Korean. The cheap end is full of momo stalls, and one pan-Asian restaurant owns the premium end.

Price benchmarks

What the same dish costs around you

Gold striped bars are my suggested price bands for you, drawn at the top of the band. Red bars are competitor prices; Red Ginger's exclude tax. Delivery-app prices for the momo chains are heavily discounted, so treat them as a floor.

Takeaway. My earlier momo price (₹220–280 for 6) was about double what the local market pays. At ₹140–180 you stay competitive with a better product. Wok dishes at ₹200–290 sit between Rice Bowl (₹170) and Red Ginger (₹290–320).

Before you sign
