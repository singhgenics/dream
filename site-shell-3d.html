What the video shows

  • A flat RCC ceiling at 4.5 m, painted white, divided into coffers by yellow downstand beams.
  • Beam A runs the full 10.5 m length, about 1.0 m in from the left wall. The strip between it and the left wall is one unbroken panel.
  • Two pillars on the right wall, P1 at about 4.8 m and P2 at about 6.8 m from the shutter. A full-width beam spans from P1. From P2 a beam spans only as far as beam A.
  • A short centre beam B joins those two cross beams, splitting that bay into two coffers.
  • A lintel ledge runs around the room at about 2.45 to 2.65 m. It sits flush with the pillar faces on the right wall and stops short of the left side of the front wall.
  • A full-height roller shutter, about 2.4 m wide, opens to roughly 4 m. A pillar and lintel beam frame it, with a solid wall section on each side.
  • One window in the back wall, centred, about 1.2 × 0.6 m, just under the ledge.
