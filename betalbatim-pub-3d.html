    Concept 23 · 4.5 × 4.5 × 10.5 m shell · 32 covers

    Dim sum, draught & records

    A light, warm, cozy pub for cabin-style Chinese, dim sum, bao, Korean bar food, craft beer and cocktails. The ground floor has a cream-and-charcoal checkerboard and brass lanterns. A reclaimed-wood wall carries vintage beer signs, and the bar glows through backlit onyx. Sand limewash, red brick and the raw concrete ceiling keep it bright. Half the seats are upstairs in an earthy 18-cover loft. Left and right are as you walk in from the road.

    Fire & life safety · security

    • StandardLaid out to the National Building Code 2016, Part 4 (Fire & Life Safety), and the usual Goa Fire NOC checklist. Click Safety above to see every device in red. The Goa Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services gives final approval.
    • ExitsFront: glass doors that open outward inside the rolling shutter, with the shutter fully open while trading. Rear: the kitchen door opens outward with a panic bar and is never padlocked during service. The farthest seat is about 18 m from the front door, well inside the 30 m limit.
    • Stair1.2 m clear, 16 risers of 149 mm, 300 mm treads, two flights of 8 with a 1.2 m landing. Wall handrail at 0.9 m, glass balustrade with a 1.0 m rail, and glow anti-slip nosings. It lands through a guarded opening in the loft floor. The under-stair booth went to make room, and the loft's full-height layout won most of those seats back (32 covers).
    • GuardsThe loft edge and landing bridge have 1.0 m rails with glass infill in place of horizontal rods, which children could climb.
    • ExtinguishersSeven in total: ABC 6 kg + CO₂ 4.5 kg at the bar, CO₂ at the main electrical board, wet-chemical (Class K) + CO₂ in the kitchen, ABC in the loft and the garden. Mounted with handles at 1–1.5 m and serviced yearly.
    • DetectionSmoke detectors at the front, the stair void, under the loft and in the loft. A heat detector in the kitchen. Manual call points and a hooter at the entrance and the loft stair head, wired to a panel by the electrical board.
    • LightingBattery emergency lights (90 min) at the entrance, the stair landing, the aisle to the kitchen and in the loft. Lit EXIT signs over the front entrance, the loft stair head and the rear door.
    • KitchenWet-chemical hood suppression with a manual pull and an automatic gas cut-off, a fire blanket by the wok range, and a first-aid box. LPG stays outside in a ventilated cage, 76 kg, under the 100 kg limit that triggers a PESO licence.
    • ElectricalThe main board (RCCB + MCBs) and fire alarm panel sit on the right wall past the keg cooler, in the open with clear space in front. Fire-retardant, low-smoke cabling, with a licensed contractor's test certificate.
    • MaterialsUpholstery, rugs, cane, the reclaimed-wood wall, dried flowers and faux plants are flame-retardant treated or certified.
    • Security5 CCTV cameras (entrance, till, loft, rear exit, garden) recording to an NVR in the locked under-stair store. A small safe under the POS. The LPG cage and empty-keg cage are locked.
    • Still to confirmWhether the authority classes the loft as a mezzanine (about 2.2 m height, one-third of floor area) or a floor. Whether a single stair is accepted for 18 loft seats. Whether an accessible toilet is required.

    Check before you commit

    • LicenceBar licenceBeer and cocktails need a Goa excise licence. Price it before you build the bar.
    • SafetyLoft for 18A steel deck holding 18 people at full tables needs a structural engineer. Ask the fire officer whether one stair is enough, or whether a second way down is required.
    • ServiceFood goes upstairsWith 16 of 30 seats in the loft, most plates climb the stair. Consider a small dumbwaiter from the kitchen pass to the loft.
    • MeasureKitchen ceiling at 2.2 m, low-profile hoodThe loft floor runs flat over the kitchen, so the hood is about 0.27 m deep (bottom 1.9 m, top 2.17 m).
