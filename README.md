# Dream

Planning workspace for a small dim sum and craft-beer pub in Betalbatim, South Goa.

## 3D models

Open either HTML file in a browser to orbit around the model.

- `index.html`: the current model as a standalone page, used for GitHub Pages
- `betalbatim-pub-3d.html`: current concept (v7). A warm loft-style, gallery-framed 30-cover dim sum and craft-beer pub in a 4.5 × 4.5 × 10.5 m shell: 8 on the ground floor (4 at the bar, 4 in the window lounge), 16 in a loft reached by a straight stair, 6 in a small beer garden, and a billing desk by the door. Click the loft or kitchen to explore them.
- `lantern-loft-3d.html`: first concept (v1), based on the earlier 6.5 m depth estimate.
