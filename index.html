Dim sum, draught & records

A warm, rustic pub serving dim sum, bar bites, craft beer and cocktails. It has exposed brick, dark timber beams, oxblood leather and a black fluted bar under Edison globes, plus a string-lit beer garden out front. Left and right are as you walk in from the road.

Zones, front to back

  • Beer gardenA timber pergola with a clear polycarbonate roof, so it works in light rain. Trailing vines and five strands of festoon bulbs hang from it over two long communal tables, with a chalkboard at the gate.
  • ReceptionOn the left as you enter: a cane-fronted host desk with a brass lamp, set against the pop-art dim sum mural. A small bench gives waiting guests somewhere to sit.
  • LoungeOn the right by the glass: a chesterfield sofa, two leather armchairs, a jukebox and plaid throws.
  • Bar3.8 m along the right wall. The counter has a black fluted front, a marble top, amber underglow and a brass foot rail. Six craft taps sit on two brass fonts. Behind it, green tile runs to the ceiling, with lit bottle shelves, two TVs, red neon tubes and a "bao, baby?" script neon. Pothos trails from a rail above.
  • BoothsTwo oxblood leather booths on the left, set against exposed brick and a wall of tin signs, clocks and plates, with Edison sconces.
  • Kitchen2.4 × 3.4 m at the back right. Staff go in from behind the bar, and servers pick up at a pass hatch. It has a wok range, fryer, a dim sum steamer station, an upright fridge, a prep table with a chiller underneath, and a pot-wash sink.
  • ExhaustA hood runs the full length of the cooking line. The duct goes out through the back wall, then up the outside to above the roof, with a cowl.
  • WashroomAt the back left, off a short corridor. Green subway tile, a timber vanity, a round brass mirror and a pendant light. A keg and dry store sits next to it.
  • LoftA steel deck at 2.55 m over the kitchen and washroom, reached by a spiral stair. Headroom up there is about 1.95 m, so it's floor-cushion seating only.

Check before you commit

  • LicenceBar licence and budgetBeer and cocktails need a Goa excise licence, which your earlier plan avoided because of its cost. Budget for it before you build the bar.
  • SafetyLoft, if you build itA 1.95 m headroom loft reached by a spiral stair may not pass fire rules for 10 guests. Phase 1 without it still gives you 31 covers and saves the steel work.
  • LicenceMusic in publicPlaying recorded music in a restaurant in India usually needs PPL and IPRS (or Novex) licences. Showing live sports needs a commercial TV subscription.
  • WeatherMonsoon gardenThe polycarbonate pergola handles drizzle, not sideways June rain. Plan on indoor seating carrying the monsoon months.
  • CheckForecourt rightsConfirm the forecourt is part of your lease and that seating there is allowed.
  • NeighboursExhaust routeThe duct is drawn going out the back wall. Check there's space behind the building for it to rise above the roof.
