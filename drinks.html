The drinks, and how to run them

Three local craft taps (a lager, the kokum gose and a rotating guest) plus Kings and Budweiser by the bottle, a short list of about ten whiskies and vodkas, eight cocktails, three zero-proof drinks with water and soft drinks, and brewed coffee. Drinks cost is tracked separately from food: aim for about 25–30% on taps and cocktails, and keep spirits at excise MRP plus your margin.

Whisky & vodka: tiered, still short

Eleven whisky lines in tiers. Four single malts (Paul John, Amrut, Glenlivet 12, Talisker 10). Four blended Scotches: Dewar's White Label and William Lawson's as the volume pours for highballs and happy hour, with Monkey Shoulder and Black Label as the step-up. Jameson is the one Irish whiskey and goes into the Irish coffee. Plus the Toki highball and a single malt flight (Paul John, Glenlivet, Talisker). Four vodkas: two premium, two infused in house.

Spirit prices are indicative. Check them against current Goa excise MRPs before printing.

Licences

The taps and cocktails need the Goa excise licence. If it isn't through on opening day, the zero-proof list covers the bar. Recorded music needs PPL and IPRS (or Novex) licences.

Coffee without a barista

A commercial filter batch brewer with two glass jugs on warming plates (Bravilor, Bunn or Marco class, about ₹35,000–60,000). It sits on the server-station counter next to the bar. The bartender loads a paper filter and ground coffee, presses one button, and gets 1.8 L (about 12 cups) in 6–7 minutes. Brew small batches and pour away anything older than 45 minutes. A small milk warmer covers "with milk". Cost per cup is about ₹15–20 with good Indian arabica, so the margin is high.

Soft drinks and packaged water: restaurants may charge above MRP for on-premise service, but keep the MRP visible on the bottle and price clearly on the menu.

Tap suppliers

Three taps, not six: at about 12–15 guests a day, six 20 L kegs would sit open for about five weeks and go stale; three turn over in about two and a half. Goa Brewing Co. (Sangolda) pours People's Lager, Susegado (Calangute) the kokum gose, and the guest tap rotates their IPA and porter with Maka Di. Ask the brewers to lend the cooler, couplers and font in return for two of the three taps. Add a fourth tap only when all three kegs empty within about 10 days. Bottled Kings and Budweiser cover budget drinkers and never go stale. Check every price against current Goa excise MRPs; craft draught at these prices runs about 30–40% beverage cost, bottles about 45–55%.

Before signing, ask each brewer: will they deliver to Betalbatim, what is the minimum order, keg size and price, which coupler type they use, whether they'll lend a kegerator or tower, and how long the unfiltered beer lasts. Each brewer's range is from public listings, so confirm what's currently in production.
