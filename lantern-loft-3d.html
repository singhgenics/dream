Lantern Loft

A warm, double-height pub for momos, craft beer and records. The front 2 m stays open to the roof as a lantern-lit void you can see from the road. A steel mezzanine covers the back 4.5 m and carries a floor-cushion lounge.

  • Bar2.6 m along the right-hand wall (as you walk in) with 4 craft taps on two brass towers. The back bar has amber-lit bottle shelves on green tile, and the end of the bar doubles as the till.

Look & feel

Lime plaster Smoked teal ceiling Timber rafters Cane Brass Green tile 2700K amber light

This is a cozy Himalayan pub on a Goan street. Keep the beams and paint the ceiling dark, so the lanterns and prayer flags glow against it. Patterned cement tiles go downstairs and warm timber upstairs. For music, play records from the vinyl corner through two speakers on the walls, and keep it at conversation volume.

Check before you commit

  • Changes the planLiquor licenceYour earlier plan skipped alcohol because a Goa bar licence would take most of the ₹10–10.5L budget. Craft beer and cocktails reverse that decision. The bar is drawn so it can open as a kombucha, mocktail and cold-brew bar, then switch to taps once you're licensed.
  • SafetyMezzanine structure and fire exitA steel deck holding 20 people needs a structural engineer's sign-off and probably landlord and panchayat approval. Ask whether one stair is enough for 20 seats upstairs, or whether the fire NOC needs a second way out.
  • MeasureHeadroomA deck at 2.5 m leaves ~2.35 m clear below and ~2.0 m upstairs at the walls. Measure the eaves and ridge on site. If the ridge is lower than ~5 m, shrink the mezzanine or drop the upstairs seats.
  • TightKitchen is ~6 m²That's enough for a ~20-dish menu with prep done off-peak. If the building has a shared toilet outside, move the washroom out and give that space to the kitchen.
  • StaffingTwo floors, one hostYour 24-seat plan had you running the floor alone. 30 seats over two levels needs a server, which fits your newer 3-staff scenario.
  • NeighboursExhaustRun the stack above the ridge and add a grease filter. Wok smoke is the most common complaint about small restaurants.
